Terminal. Two colored-pencil panels on warm paper explain the rewards and risks of hacking.
Left, ‘Hold J with empty hands to hack’: an orange kitten operates a terminal with cyan screens on a vehicle roof. An arrow below the hacking progress bar points to three possible disruptions: a car getting dirty, driving away, or exploding. Captions read ‘Randomly disrupt the enemy's car’ and ‘Unleash chaos on the enemy's car!’.
Right, ‘You're vulnerable while hacking!’: a gray rival slaps the hacking kitten away from the terminal; a cross on the screen indicates interruption. The caption reads ‘Time it right—watch out for slaps!’.
